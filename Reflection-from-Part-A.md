What is agentc? It is a practice of designing, building, testing, and maintaining software in which AI can act and decide for themselves automatically.

How deos AI agent differ from a traditional software application? Traditional software follows fixed rules and decisions predetermined by the code owner, AI logic decieds based on logic and a facts. It also acts independently only on its own mind and what it thinks is the right answer for a scenario.

Why are agile methods appropriate for developing agentic systems? Methods such as scrum use short sprints, frequent feedback, and continuous adaption. Teams can test the agent in small increments, observe and fix problems early on.

What risks should developers consider when deploying autonomous AI agents? Developers should consider privacy, output correctness, bias avoidance, and no unintentional unethical actions the AI would do on its own.

Why is human oversight important in AI-enabled applications?
Human oversight can help provide the context whether the action an AI has taken is unethical, provide accountability and correct wrong decisions and mistakes the model has made for it improve further and make less mistake and soon be reliable.
